# TradeTrack AI v27 — Google Drive Screenshots

- Setup and after-trade screenshots upload to Google Drive.
- A private Drive folder is used, with one subfolder per Trade ID.
- Google Sheet stores the Before Screenshot URL and After Screenshot URL.
- History cards show View setup screenshot / View after screenshot buttons.
- Clicking a screenshot button opens the corresponding Google Drive file.
- Manual trades still save structured trade/checklist data to Google Sheets when cloud sync is configured.
- India/INR and Forex/USD are kept separate in the dashboard.
